Our annual resume workshop brought together members and alumni for an evening of red pens, hard truths, and (hopefully) much stronger resumes.

Alumni volunteers sat down one-on-one with members to go line by line through formatting, bullet points, and that one summary section everyone insists on keeping even though nobody reads it.

A few common notes from the night: quantify your impact wherever possible, cut anything from high school, and please, for the love of all that is holy, proofread before you hit submit.

If you missed this one, don't worry — we'll be running another session before the fall recruiting season kicks off.
