Delta Chapter’s trip to the National Executive Council (NEC) in Spring 2026 to St. Cloud, Minnesota was a lot of fun and very informative. Representatives learned about the inner workings of how our fraternity operates on a national level while connecting with other chapters. New legislation was introduced, potential new chapters were discussed, and new bonds were forged while visiting our brothers at St. Cloud State University. 

After spending all day attending to fraternity matters, all NEC attendees went out to dinner and let the formalities of the day fall away. The time spent together afterwards brought us all together, where we learned the importance of refuting taxation without representation and having an extra pair of shoes.
