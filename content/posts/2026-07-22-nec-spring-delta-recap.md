This spring, a group of our members traveled to represent Delta Chapter at the National Executive Council meeting, connecting with sister chapters from across the country.

Between panel discussions on chapter growth and late-night conversations in the hotel lobby, it was a great reminder of how much bigger our organization is than just our little corner of campus.

We came back with a handful of new ideas for events, a couple of new friends in other chapters, and a lot of enthusiasm for the rest of the semester.

Thanks to everyone who helped fundraise so we could make the trip — we couldn't have done it without you.
