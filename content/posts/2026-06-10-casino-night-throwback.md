With finals season behind us, we're looking back at one of our favorite socials of the spring semester — Casino Night!

Members traded in fake chips at the poker and blackjack tables, fought over the last slice of pizza, and somehow ended up in an hour-long argument about whether the dealer was rigging the roulette wheel (he wasn't).

Events like this are what make the chapter feel like a second home, not just a professional organization. Big thanks to the social committee for pulling it off.

Keep an eye out for next semester's date — we're already planning round two.
